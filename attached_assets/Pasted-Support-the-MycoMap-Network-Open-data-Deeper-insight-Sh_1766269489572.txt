Support the MycoMap Network

Open data. Deeper insight. Shared infrastructure.

MycoMap is committed to open fungal biodiversity data. All core observations, sequences, maps, and base datasets remain freely accessible to everyone.

Membership support makes it possible for us to go further: validating records, synthesizing large datasets into meaningful patterns, maintaining sequencing pipelines, and sharing expert interpretation in near real time.

Membership is not about restricting access — it’s about supporting the infrastructure that turns open data into understanding.

What Membership Supports

Member support helps sustain:

Community DNA barcoding of fungal specimens

Validation and annotation of novel, rare, and range-extending records

Advanced analytical tools that synthesize open data into higher-order insights

Live discovery monitoring for new and notable records

Expert interpretation of sequencing results, methods, and trends

Transparent communication about what is happening in the lab and field

Universal Benefits (All Membership Levels)

All monthly supporters receive:

Full access to MycoMap’s supporter-only Research Notes
(published via Substack; focused on sequencing, interpretation, and methods)

Invitation to a monthly live video update + Q&A
Covering recent discoveries, pipeline updates, and what’s coming next

Optional recognition on our Supporters & Collaborators page
(or listed as “Anonymous” if preferred)

Membership Levels
Tier 1 — Community Supporter

$5–$10 / month

A simple way to support open fungal biodiversity research.

Includes:

All universal benefits

Helps underwrite DNA barcoding for ~25 modern macrofungal specimens per year in the community pipeline

If you do collect, you may allocate this capacity to your own properly prepared specimens
(subject to submission guidelines and available capacity)

2–3× per year Field Notes emails with highlights and behind-the-scenes updates

This tier is ideal for supporters who want to help sustain the work without needing regular hands-on involvement.

Tier 2 — Network Supporter

$15–$25 / month

For those who want a closer connection to the work and its direction.

Includes:

All Tier 1 benefits

Helps underwrite DNA barcoding for ~100 modern macrofungal specimens per year

Option to apply this capacity to your own specimens, if you collect

Monthly Lab Notebook mini-digest
(3–5 concise bullets summarizing what happened in the lab and field that month)

Occasional member surveys or topic voting to help shape which regions, taxa, or questions we prioritize next

This tier supports both infrastructure and shared scientific direction.

Tier 3 — Sustaining Supporter

$50–$100+ / month

For individuals, groups, or institutions providing major ongoing support.

Includes:

All Tier 2 benefits

Helps underwrite an open-ended volume of community barcoding
(no preset annual cap, within reason and capacity)

If you or your group collect heavily, we’ll work with you to plan how your support can cover a substantial share of your material

Priority placement in the community barcoding queue, as capacity allows

Annual impact snapshot summarizing what your support made possible
(approximate specimens processed, regions represented, and unique taxa or barcode clusters)

Invitation to an annual small-group Deep Dive session (Zoom) focused on data, pipelines, and upcoming work

This tier directly sustains sequencing capacity, expert time, and long-term infrastructure.

Advanced Tools & Member-Supported Features

All underlying data remain open. Membership support enables access to higher-order synthesis tools, including:

Advanced discovery and search tools
Rich filtering across geography, taxonomy, substrate, seasonality, and clade

Live Research & Validation Stream
Real-time updates on validated observations, including:

First state and regional records

Range extensions

Notable taxonomic or barcode clusters

Expert annotations and context

Customizable monitoring
Members can highlight taxa or regions of personal or professional interest

These tools are designed to complement open data by making large datasets interpretable and actionable.

A Note on Open Access

MycoMap exists to expand knowledge, not to restrict it.

All raw observations, sequences, and baseline maps remain open access.
Membership support funds expert validation, synthesis, interpretation, and the computational infrastructure that makes those insights possible.
